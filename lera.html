Remember: in an indirect question the word order is like a statement. No do / does / did, no inversion.
Yes/no questions need if or whether.
One tense, one rule:
Present simple: Where do you live? → Do you know where you live?
Past simple: What did she say? → Can you tell me what she said?
Present perfect: How long have you been here? → I wonder how long you have been here.
Future: When will it start? → Do you know when it will start?
Modals: Where can I buy a ticket? → Could you tell me where I can buy a ticket?
