🎀 Grisha's grammar box

will / won't + глагол (без to) — для решений «прямо сейчас», предложений помощи и обещаний.

💡 decision — решаешь прямо сейчас:
This top is so cute! I'll buy it. · I won't go out tonight.
🤝 offer — предлагаешь помочь:
I'll carry your bag. · Shall I open the window? · Shall we go to Turkey?
🤞 promise — обещаешь:
I'll always love you, Grisha. · I won't tell anybody.
⚠️ НЕ Present Simple: I help you with those bags → I'll help you with those bags.

1. Complete with will / won't ✍️

Поставь глагол в скобках в форму 'll / will или won't.

1A Grisha is meowing — I think he's hungry!
B Don't worry, I (feed) him.
2A The pink dress or the white one?
B Hmm… I (take) the pink one, of course!
3A Please don't tell anybody my secret!
B I promise I (not tell) anybody.
4A My suitcase for Turkey is so heavy!
B I (help) you with it.
5A It's minus 25 in Chelyabinsk today!
B Brr! I (not go) out. I (stay) at home with Grisha.
6A Oh no, I forgot my ballet shoes!
B No problem. I (lend) you mine.
7A Our flight to Thailand is at 6 a.m.!
B OK, I promise I (not be) late!
