Past Simple или Past Continuous? 🤔

Past Simple
(факт, случилось)		I bought new gloves. / I lost my scarf.
Маркеры: yesterday, last winter, two days ago, in 2025
Past Continuous
(процесс в момент)		I was wearing my coat. / It was snowing.
was / were + глагол-ing
Маркеры: at 5 o'clock yesterday, at that moment, all evening, while

Процесс + его прервали ⚡

Длинное действие (процесс) — Past Continuous, короткое, которое его прервало — Past Simple.
Thomas was sleeping when I came into the room. 🐱
Mum was cooking pasta when Dad came home. 🍝
