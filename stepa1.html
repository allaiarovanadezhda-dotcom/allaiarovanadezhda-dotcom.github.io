5. Past Continuous 🌙👑

Поставь глагол в скобках в правильную форму.

Past Continuous = что происходило в какой-то момент в прошлом
I / he / she / it was + глагол-ing · we / you / they were + глагол-ing
❗ Короткое действие, которое «врезалось» — в Past Simple:
I was watching TV when my cat jumped on the sofa.
  1. Yesterday at 6 pm I (dance) at my folk dance class.
  2. My rats (sleep) when the cat came into the room. 🐀
  3. What (you / do) at 9 o'clock last night? — I (watch) "The Magnificent Century".
  4. Mum (make) dinner when the mini-pig ran into the kitchen.
  5. The turtle (not / swim) — it (eat) a salad. 🐢
  6. While we were lying on the beach in the Maldives, we (see) a big sea turtle!
  7. My dogs (bark) all night, so I (not / sleep) well.
