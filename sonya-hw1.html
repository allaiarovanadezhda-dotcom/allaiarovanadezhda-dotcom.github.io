⏰ 5 времён — шпаргалка

      
Ищи слово-подсказку в предложении — оно говорит, какое время нужно!

      
🔁 Present Simple — обычно, всегда

        
I / you / we / they wear · he / she / it wears (+s!)

        
➖ don't / doesn't wear · ❓ Do / Does … wear?

        
usually, always, often, never, every day, on Mondays

        
Sonya usually wears leggings at gymnastics.

      
▶️ Present Continuous — прямо сейчас

        
am / is / are + -ing

        
➖ isn't wearing · ❓ Is she wearing…?

        
now, right now, at the moment, Look! Listen! Shh!

        
Look! Timofey is wearing my pink scarf!

      
⏪ Past Simple — было и закончилось

        
глагол + -ed или 2-я форма (wore, bought, went)

        
➖ didn't + глагол · ❓ Did … + глагол?

        
yesterday, last week, last summer, two days ago, in 2024

        
Last summer we went to China.

      
✅ Present Perfect — уже / ещё не / когда-нибудь (результат сейчас)

        
have / has + 3-я форма (worn, bought, lost, been)

        
➖ haven't / hasn't · ❓ Have you … ?

        
already, just, yet, ever, never

        
I have already cooked pancakes! Have you ever been to China?

      
🔮 Future Simple — будет

        
will + глагол (для всех!)

        
➖ won't · ❓ Will you … ?

        
tomorrow, next week, next Saturday, soon, I think…

        
Tomorrow I will wear my floral dress.

      
⚠️ Главная ловушка: yesterday / ago / last → Past Simple, а already / just / ever / never → Present Perfect.
