📌 must / have to — шпаргалка

      
✅ must — должна (я сама так считаю, правило)

        
I / you / he / she must + глагол (без to!)

        
I must practise more. You must wear a helmet when you skateboard.

      
📋 have to / has to — приходится, так надо (обязанность)

        
I / you / we / they have to · he / she / it has to

        
❓ Do you have to…? · Does she have to…?

        
Dan has to travel a lot. Do you have to train every day?

      
⛔ mustn't — НЕЛЬЗЯ!

        
You mustn't touch the hot oven! Dogs mustn't eat chocolate.

      
😌 don't have to / doesn't have to — НЕ ОБЯЗАТЕЛЬНО (можно, но не нужно)

        
It's Saturday — I don't have to go to school. Megan doesn't have to have special training.

      
⏪ had to — пришлось (в прошлом, для всех)

        
Last year Megan had to visit the dentist six times.

      
⚠️ Главная ловушка: mustn't = нельзя, а don't have to = не обязательно. Это разные вещи!

    

  

  

    

      
💬 Как сказать «…, поэтому я …»

      
I love + хобби, so I'm + качество

        
I love doing gymnastics, so I'm fit and determined.
Timofey likes playing chess, so he's patient.
I enjoy cooking, so I'm creative.

        
После love / like / enjoy глагол с -ing: cooking, painting, skateboarding
